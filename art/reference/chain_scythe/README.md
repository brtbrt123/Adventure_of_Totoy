# Chain Scythe + Reaper's Coil (reference art, v1)

Status: **approved by the human (v1.1)** and imported into `art/src` on 2026-10-20 (see "In-game sprites" at the end). Not wired into the game yet, not committed.
Damage, reach, timing, cost, cooldown and stagger length of the swings and the right-click skill are owner/core decisions. Nothing here sets them.

Generated by `python tools/art/make_chain_scythe_art.py` (this folder) and `python tools/art/export_chain_scythe_cutin.py` (`presentation/cutin/chainscythe_*`). Both use Pillow only and fixed seeds. Two runs were checked and give byte-identical files.
Everything is Totoy 36 with hard alpha. Every PNG is checked by `artlib.check_png` (or the cut-in `check_hard`) plus a reserved-colour check. Gold (`x`,`y`) and red (`z`,`A`) are not used.

Totoy's bodies are the in-game **leaning** frames, built the same way as the dagger set: `import_dagger_sprites.build_dagger_frame` takes the upright `hero2_*` frame, applies the `make_hero_lean_sheet` warp and removes the katana. Idle stays upright. Two poses use their own lean of the upright frame through `make_hero_lean_sheet.build_frame` with a custom `Pose`: the charged spin leans forward 8 deg (it is built from victory_3, which is otherwise upright), and the pull leans back -14 deg.

Colour language (spectral teal/green steel; violet belongs to the katana and pale blue to the daggers):
- Haft: `h` stone_4, with `t` teal_1 ferrules and a `j` pommel ring.
- Blade: `u` teal_2 body, `t` teal_1 spine, `1` cream cutting edge on the concave side, `p` water_6 tip. Ink outline `0`.
- Chain: links alternate between face-on and edge-on. Face-on links are 2 px across in `j`/`1` with `u`/`t`. Edge-on links are 1 px in `u`/`t`, with a dark joint. The pattern repeats every 4 px and has an ink outline.
- Effects: `1` cream, `p` water_6, `7` grass_6 (green glint), `u`, `t`, and `k` water_1 (deep blue-black shadow and rim).

## v1.1 fixes (after the human's first review)

1. **Grips ("his fist is empty").** Each pose now builds the scythe from the measured hand points (`scythe_geometry`), so the haft runs exactly through the gripping fist(s). Two-handed poses: swing 1, swing 2 and recovery. One fist on the haft plus one on the chain: ready and windup. One fist on the haft: walk and swing 3 (on swing 3 both fists are on the haft at the same point).
   Then `fist_over` redraws a closed fist on top of the weapon. The fist is 3 px across and 2 px along the haft or chain, with lit knuckles, a shaded underside and ink only where nothing else is drawn, so the haft visibly enters and leaves the fist.
   Every hand that holds the chain gets the same treatment: ready (chain loop in the free fist), spin (the raised fist holds the chain), windup, whip, and pull (the taut chain runs through both fists). The cut-in poses are rebuilt from these, so their grips are fixed too.
   The katana arm poses allowed all of these grips, so no arms were redrawn.
2. **Reach and contact ("he is not hitting the enemy").**
   - The arcs now end about 30-34 px in front of the feet: swing 1 rx 33, swing 2 rx 30, swing 3 r 24 centred 6 px ahead. The spin ring is rx 34, ry 11.
   - In `combo_sequence.gif` the golem's front edge stands 22 px ahead of Totoy, inside that reach. On every swing's peak frame the golem gets a hit flash (lit pixels go cream/pale for one frame), a stagger star on the point where the arc crosses it, and a 2-3 px recoil into the hurt frame, then a clink ring.
   - In the spin the scythe circles on its chain. Each time it passes through the golem's side, the golem flashes and recoils and a burst appears at the blade.
   - In `skill_sequence.gif` the blade sinks into the golem's chest (hooked scythe drawn over the golem), with the clink and flash on the golem. The golem is then dragged until its front edge is 13 px from Totoy's feet (adjacent). The stagger burst sits on its chest, then dizzy stars.

## Known limits

- These are single key poses, not full animation sets. The swings have no separate windup or active frames yet. A runtime set (idle 4, walk 6, attack 6 x 3 ...) would follow the dagger importer's pattern.
- Grips follow the hand points the dagger set measured (about +-1 px). The bodies keep the katana's arm poses. The fist is a generic 3x2 block redrawn over the weapon, not hand-painted fingers.
- The hit flash recolours the golem's light pixels, and the golem is mostly light stone, so the flash frame reads as a nearly white golem.
- The combo GIF puts the golem very close (22 px) so that every swing visibly connects. Real spacing and hitboxes are core decisions.
- At 64x64 the haft is about 30-38 px (as tall as Totoy) and the 1px haft is thin at 1x. On dark ground it reads through the stone_4 core; on grass, through the ink outline.
- The wavy chain link (`fx_chain_link_3`) does not tile perfectly; the taut ones do.
- `whip` and `pull` cells cut the chain at the cell edge. The skill GIF draws the chain between hand and golem at runtime-like positions (helper `chain_between`).
- The skill GIF uses `golem2_idle_1` / `golem2_hurt_1` as is. Distances and timings in both GIFs are illustrative only.
- The preview banner gradient, veils, title and subtitle are drawn the way the runtime script would draw them, so they are not palette art (as in the other cut-ins).
- Whether it looks good, feels fast enough or reads in motion is the human's call. Only still sheets and the GIFs were checked.

## In-game sprites (imported 2026-10-20)

Generated by `python tools/art/import_chain_scythe_sprites.py` (then `validate_art.py`, `build_art.py`, `preview_art.py`). Deterministic: two runs give identical files. It reuses this folder's drawing code (`make_chain_scythe_art.py`) and the dagger importer's bodies (`import_dagger_sprites.py`: the leaning `hero2_*` bodies, katana removed, the measured hand points carried through the lean). Machine-readable list: `art/build/chain_scythe_sprites_manifest.json` (per group: frame count, size, anchor per frame, `body_sources`, `hands`, and `chain_origin` for the Reaper's Coil whip/pull frames; hands and chain_origin are relative to the anchor).

**Hero frames.** Visual id `totoy_scythe`. The numbering is exactly `hero2_*`'s, so wiring can swap the prefix `hero2_` -> `totoy_scythe_`. Every anchor is the feet point of the `hero2_*` frame the body comes from. The canvas grows where the scythe reaches past it, and the anchor moves with it, so the feet line up (checked for every frame: the feet rows are pixel-identical relative to the anchor). The bodies lean like the katana set: idle and victory are upright. Right-facing only; mirror around the anchor for left. The approved key poses come out pixel-identical to `concept_v1.png`: ready = idle 1, swing_1/2/3 = attack1/2/3 frame 4, windup = coil_windup_1, recovery = attack1_6. `walk_carry` = walk 2 differs only in the chain link phase.
