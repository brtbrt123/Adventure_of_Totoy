# Spirit Lantern + Phantom Cut (reference art, v1)

Status: **for the human's review only.** Nothing has been imported into `art/src` or wired into the game, and nothing is committed. Duration, pulse rate, radius, heal and Edge amounts, echo delay, damage and cost are owner/core decisions. Nothing here sets them.

Generated by `python tools/art/make_aspect_lantern_phantom_art.py` (Pillow only, fixed seeds). Two runs give byte-identical files.
Everything is Totoy 36 with hard alpha. Translucency is faked with checkerboard or ordered dither. Every sheet is checked by `artlib.check_png` plus a reserved-colour check. Gold (`x`,`y`) and red (`z`,`A`) are not used. Every frame's width and height is a multiple of 8, so it can go through `validate_art.py` unchanged when it is imported.

Colour language:
- **Spirit Lantern** (cool-warm spectral): an old stone lantern (stone ramp `g h i j`, ink `0`) with a teal-white flame. The flame core is `1` cream, then `p` water_6 and `o` water_5, inside `u` teal_2 / `t` teal_1 glass, with a `v` lilac tip flicker and lilac smoke. Heal motes are teal and cream, because the HUD's HP bar is teal `t`. Edge sparks are cream with lilac tails, because the HUD's Edge bar is lilac `v`.
- **Phantom Cut**: a lilac `v` outline, a checkerboard body in `o`/`p` (pale blue-white), and the blade in `p` with `1` glints. The echo arcs have a `1`/`p` cutting edge, an `o` body, dithered `n` inside, a `v` rim and dotted `v` trailing streaks.
- Totoy 36 has only one lilac (`v` #8a6fa8). "Pale lilac" is therefore built from `v` next to `o`/`p` (outline plus pale fill), not from a separate pale-lilac colour.

Suggested timeline: casting turns the overlay and the marker on (marker frame 1 can be replaced by a small pop, as in the GIF). The next basic attack turns both off when its active frame starts. A moment (about 0.2-0.4 s) after the real hit, play ghost 1-4 together with echo 1-4 (echo 2 is the phantom hit) and spark 1-5 on the target. In the GIF the real hit is on attack frame 4 and the phantom hit lands about 385 ms later (illustrative).

**GIF (`phantom_cut_sequence.gif`)**: three rounds, one per swing, so that all three echoes are shown (in the game only the next attack echoes). Its echo arc sweeps through the golem, which flashes and recoils again under the ghost spark. The golem's front edge is 22 px from Totoy's feet, as in the Chain Scythe GIF.

## 3. Icons

`ui_aspect_spirit_lantern_1` shows a lit stone lantern on a teal pool. `ui_aspect_phantom_cut_1` shows a bright katana cut with its dithered lilac echo a step behind. Both are 24x24 with anchor (12,12). The ring is copied from `ui_aspect_gale_cut_1`, as were the dagger and scythe icons. `ui_aspect_spirit_lantern_lit_1..4` is an optional flicker of the lantern icon for the HUD while the lantern burns, used if a "lantern active" cue is wanted on the slot.
**No 48px versions**: the existing Aspect icons are 24px only. The character menu's Aspect slots draw the 24px icon (`character_menu.gd`, `_ui(Hud.ASPECT_ICONS[i], ... 24x24)`), and only weapons have 48px medallions.

## Suggested in-game names (when imported)

These are the sprite names above, unchanged (`fx_lantern_*`, `fx_phantom_*`, `ui_aspect_spirit_lantern_1`, `ui_aspect_phantom_cut_1`). The charge overlays could be renamed `fx_phantom_charge_<state>_<n>_<phase>`, as they are now, or generated at import for every katana frame that needs them.

## Known limits

- The pool radius (58x24 px) and the ghost offset (8 px) are illustrative. The real radius and offset come from core tuning, and the pool may need a scaled or regenerated decal (`PRX`/`PRY` in the script).
- The pool's dashed rim reads like an area marker. That is deliberate (you can see where the light ends), but it is a style choice to judge.
- The lantern has no "carry" or "set down" pose on Totoy. In the GIF he just idles while it drops in at his feet.
- The charge overlay follows only the blade's pixels (the katana is the water ramp on `hero2_*`). It does not wrap the arm. On frames where the blade is mostly hidden behind the body, the glow is small.
- The phantom ghosts are recolours of single hero frames, so their silhouettes match the real swing exactly. The "form" and "fragments" frames are sparse and read mainly as motion, not as a figure.
- Over the golem's grey stone the ghost's pale body has less contrast than over grass or the dark floor. The echo arc and spark carry the hit there.
- The mock HUD bars, the golem flash and the hit star on the real swing in the GIFs are for viewing only.

## What I could not judge

Whether the two Aspects look good, feel distinct from Gale Cut and Moon Cleave (Moon Cleave is also a crescent), whether the lantern reads as "spirit" rather than plain, whether the timing feels right in motion, and whether the dither on the ghost reads as translucent on the real arena floor and at the real window scale are all for the human to decide. Only the still sheets, crops at 1x-4x and the GIF frames were checked.
