# Spirit Lantern + Phantom Cut (reference art v2, imported)

Status: **v2, made from the human's review of v1, and imported into `art/src` as in-game sprites. Not wired into the game and not committed.** v1 (the reviewed version) is kept in `v1/`. Duration, pulse rate, radius, heal and Edge amounts, echo delay, damage and cost are owner/core decisions. Nothing here sets them; the art follows the numbers the human gave (light radius about 44 px, Phantom Cut a 5 s buff).

What changed from v1:
1. **Bigger light pool.** The pool now covers the gameplay radius of about 44 px: an ellipse rx 44 / ry 20 (88x40 px on screen, with the same floor squash as before), on a 96x48 canvas with the anchor (48,24). The pulse rings scale with it. On the strong frames (pulse 2-3) the ring has a 2 px leading edge, and a trailing ripple follows it out to pulse 4. Same frame counts. The lantern prop, motes, sparks and icons are unchanged.
2. **Phantom Cut is a 5 s buff, and every attack leaves an echo.** The glow is now a sustained buff overlay. It exists for every hero frame the game animates while the buff can run: idle, walk, dash, parry, hurt and attack windup/active/recovery, for the katana, the dagger and the scythe. There are 6 shimmer phases per frame, with three rising motes instead of two. New: a buff start burst (5 frames) and a buff end fade (4). The dagger (4 swings) and scythe (3 swings) get ghosts and echo arcs. The dagger arcs are short, fast slashes. The scythe arcs are the in-game `fx_scythe_arc_*` crescents recoloured, so the echo has the scythe's own shape. The katana arcs are the v1 arcs. The "echo pending" marker is kept; it now shows while the buff runs.
3. The heal motes and Edge sparks were renamed to `fx_lantern_motes_*` / `fx_lantern_spark_*`, the marker to `fx_phantom_marker_*`, and the katana ghost/echo to `fx_phantom_ghost_katana_*` / `fx_phantom_arc_katana_*`.

Generated by `python tools/art/make_aspect_lantern_phantom_art.py` (Pillow only, fixed seeds; two runs give byte-identical files). Imported by `python tools/art/import_lantern_phantom_sprites.py`, which runs the same `build()`, so the review sheets and `art/src` hold the same pixels. Everything uses Totoy 36 with hard alpha and no gold `x`,`y` or red `z`,`A`. Translucency is faked with checkerboard or ordered dither.

## In-game sprites (for the wiring task)

All are in `art/src`, built into `art/build/sprites.png/.json`. The manifest is `art/build/lantern_phantom_sprites_manifest.json` (group -> frame count, size per frame, anchor per frame and its meaning, suggested ms, ghost sources). Frames are `<group>_1..N`. Anchors are the point placed on the world position. Mirror around the anchor for a left-facing hero, as with the hero frames (`ArenaAnimation.destination`). **940 sprites:** lantern 44, glow 792, other Phantom 98, icons 6.

### Phantom Cut (890)

| Sprite | Frames | Size | Anchor | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fx_phantom_glow_<hero frame>_N` | 6 per hero frame, 132 hero frames | cropped per frame | the hero frame's feet point in the crop | **buff glow**, see the naming rule below |
| `fx_phantom_start_N` | 5 | 48x48 | (24,44) Totoy's feet | buff start burst over the hero; the glow starts with frame 1 |
| `fx_phantom_end_N` | 4 | 48x48 | (24,44) | buff end fade over the hero; the glow and marker stop at frame 1 |
| `fx_phantom_ghost_<weapon>_<swing>_N` | 4 each: katana 1-3, dagger 1-4, scythe 1-3 | same as the source attack frame | same as the source | the phantom: `<set>_attack<swing>_right_<3,4,4,5>` recoloured (form, full, fade, fragments). Draw at Totoy's feet + **8 px along his facing** (`ghost_offset_px`), mirrored with him |
| `fx_phantom_arc_<weapon>_<swing>_N` | 4 each (lead, peak, follow, fragments) | katana and scythe 96x64, dagger 64x48 | katana and scythe (40,56), dagger (16,44): the phantom's feet | the echo slash over the ghost; phase 2 is the phantom hit. About 35/55/55/70 ms |
| `fx_phantom_spark_N` | 5 | 32x32 | (16,16) hit point | where an echo lands; start on arc phase 2 |
| `fx_phantom_marker_N` | 4 | 16x16 | (8,15) bottom centre | "echo pending" cue 3 px above Totoy's head while the buff runs, about 120 ms |

`<weapon>` is `katana` (hero set `hero2`), `dagger` (`totoy_dagger`) or `scythe` (`totoy_scythe`).

**Glow naming rule.** For the hero frame `F` the game is drawing (for example `hero2_attack2_right_3`, `totoy_dagger_walk_right_5`, `totoy_scythe_parry_right_1`), the overlay for shimmer phase `p` is `"fx_phantom_glow_%s_%d" % [F, p]`. The phase is `(ticks since the buff started / 5) % 6 + 1` (about 80 ms at 60 Hz) and does not depend on the hero frame. Draw it right after the hero frame at the same position with the same mirroring, using its own `ax`/`ay` from `sprites.json`. Glow overlays are cropped to the glow, so their size differs from the hero frame, but the anchors line up. Covered: `idle_right_1-4`, `walk_right_1-6`, `dash_right_1-4`, `parry_right_1-6`, `hurt_right_1-4` and `attack<s>_right_1-6` of `hero2` (s 1-3), `totoy_dagger` (s 1-4) and `totoy_scythe` (s 1-3). **Not covered:** `victory`, the Reaper's Coil poses `totoy_scythe_coil_*` and the Lightning set `totoy_lightning_*`. When the name is missing from `sprites.json`, draw no glow.

Suggested timeline: on cast, start 1-5, glow on and marker on. For 5 s, each swing's real hit is followed after a short delay by ghost 1-4 and arc 1-4 of that weapon and swing, and spark 1-5 on the target (echoes may overlap the next swing). When the buff ends, glow off, marker off and end 1-4.

## Known limits

- The glow follows the weapon's metal by colour: the katana is `k l`, the dagger steel `j n o p`, and the scythe blade and chain `1 t u p j`. The dagger's painted swing trails (`n o p`) and the scythe's chain glow as well. Grips, guards and the scythe haft stay plain. A few stray `j` pixels on the hero bodies can catch a glow pixel. The glow does not wrap the arm or body. On frames where the weapon is mostly hidden, the glow is small.
- The ghosts are recolours of single hero frames, so their silhouettes match the real swing exactly. The "form" and "fragments" frames are sparse and read as motion, not as a figure.
- The dagger arcs are new shapes: twin thrust cuts, a rising backhand, a crossed X and a big downward finisher. They were drawn to the dagger poses, not taken from an in-game dagger effect, because the dagger has no arc sprites.
- The pool's dashed rim reads as an area marker, which is deliberate. At 88 px across it covers more of the arena floor.
- The mock HUD bars, the buff timer bar, the golem flash and the real-hit star in the GIFs are for viewing only.

## What I could not judge

Whether the bigger pool looks right on the real arena floor at the real window scale. Whether the 6-phase glow reads as a sustained buff and not as noise during fast combos. Whether the start and end effects are strong enough. Whether the dagger's short echo slashes and the recoloured scythe crescents read as echoes and not as real hits. The overall feel and timing in motion. Only the still sheets, crops at 1x-4x and the GIF frames were checked.
