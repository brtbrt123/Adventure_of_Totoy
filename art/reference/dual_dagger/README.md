# Dual Dagger + Lightning Form (reference art, v1)

Status: concept and reference layers for the human's approval. **Not imported into `art/src`, not wired into the game.**
Damage, timing, cost, cooldown and hit count of the right-click skill are owner/core decisions; nothing here sets them.

Generated by `python tools/art/make_dual_dagger_art.py` (Pillow only, fixed seeds, re-running gives identical files).
Everything is drawn procedurally in the Totoy 36 palette with hard alpha. Gold (`x`,`y`) and red (`z`,`A`) are not used.
Totoy's body comes from the in-game `hero2_*` frames with the baked-in katana removed, so he stays the same character.

Lightning colours: `p` water_6 (white core), `o` water_5, `n` water_4, `m` water_3, `l` water_2, `k` water_1 (dark rim), `v` lilac (stray sparks).
Daggers: blade `j` stone_6 / `i` stone_5, edge glint `o`, tip `p`, guard `f`/`g`/`h`, grip/pommel `e` plus the hero's fist.

All atlases are 1x game pixels. The `preview_*` and `*sequence*` files are scaled and only for viewing.

## Suggested in-game names (when imported later)

- Body without a weapon: `hero2_unarmed_<pose>`. The current `hero2_*` frames have the katana baked in, so a dagger runtime needs these frames, or the hero re-imported without a weapon.
- Daggers: `wpn_dual_dagger_back_<pose>`, `wpn_dual_dagger_front_<pose>`.
- Lightning body: `hero2_lightning_<pose>`. Charged body: `hero2_charged_<pose>`.
- Aura: `fx_lightning_aura_back_1..10`, `fx_lightning_aura_front_1..10`. Loop at about 60-70 ms per frame; the back and front frames with the same number belong together. This matches the convention of `fx_black_aura_*` (64x64, anchor 32,56).
- Afterimages: `fx_lightning_afterimage_blink_1..3`, `fx_lightning_afterimage_slash_1..3`.
- Cuts: `fx_lightning_slash_<e|se|s|sw|w|nw|n|ne>_<1..4>`. The direction is the cut's direction of travel (0 deg = east, clockwise, screen y down). The phases are lead, peak, follow and fragments; suggested timing is about 33/50/50/66 ms.
- Hit sparks: `fx_lightning_hit_1..6` (about 40 ms each). Chain bolts: `fx_lightning_chain_1..4`. Rotate or stretch these between two points, or chain them end to end.
- Icons: `ui_aspect_lightning_form_1` (24px skill), `ui_weapon_dual_dagger_1` (48px weapon).

Importing means writing text grids into `art/src/` with `# anchor:` headers. Each 64x64 cell is already a multiple of 8, so the validator accepts it.

## Known limits

- The poses reuse the katana animation bodies, so the arms are posed for a two-handed sword, not true dual-wield choreography. The far hand's position is estimated on some frames (`ready_2`, `slash_3`).
- The lightning palette has only the water ramp plus cream and lilac. Pure white is not available; water_6 `#e0f0ec` is the brightest.
- Bolts are 1px lines. They read at 4x but are thin at 1x on light grass, so the cuts get a dark `k` rim.

## Shared cut-in format (approved 2026-10-19: "this will be our cutout art format")

Every form cut-in (Lightning Form, Black Form, later ones) uses the structure below. Exporters: `tools/art/export_lightning_cutin.py` (the reference; it owns the shared helpers) and `tools/art/export_black_form_cutin.py` (imports them). Players: `presentation/arena/lightning_form_cutin.gd` and `presentation/arena/black_form_cutin.gd` (parallel scripts with the same timeline and API).

- Canvas 800x450, drawn at 0.4 on the 320x180 game canvas, nearest filtering. All PNGs are Totoy 36 with hard alpha, no gold/red, drawn at 1x and scaled up with integer nearest-neighbour.
- Files `<form>_pose_<crouch|ready|burst>.png` (lit pose: rim light on the body, 1px dark gap, coloured rim with a ramp falloff, small effect shapes off the rim) and `_sil.png` (ink silhouette, cream line, coloured outline: the impact frame); `<form>_aura.png` (12 frames, 6x2 atlas, 384x448 cells, bottom-centred, behind the figure); 4 backdrop effect frames 800x450 (lead, strike, afterglow, fragments; the strike breaks out of the banner over the whole screen); `<form>_flash_1..3.png` 800x450 (the pop, dense speed lines, thinning lines); `<form>_cutin.json` (pose `w/h/pad/inner_w/inner_h/feet_x`, aura frames, file lists, timings); untracked review aids `<form>_cutin_preview.gif` and `<form>_cutin_contact.png`.
- Timeline (preview seconds, played at SPEED 0.85, about 1.25 s of frozen world): pop 0-0.167, banner slam 0.03-0.17 (ease-back), crouch 0.10, cut to ready 0.37, cut to burst 0.60 (one impact-silhouette frame on a pale banner with radial lines, then one veiled frame), effect strike sequence 1,2,2,3,3,4,4 then 3/4 flicker, title slam 0.66 in the pixel font with the real numbers as a subtitle, banner exit 0.88-1.04 with speed-line smears.
- Per form: colour language, the effect shapes, the title and subtitle, the exit direction and the pose source. Lightning: pale blue/cream bolts, poses from the 64px dagger concept at x12, exits right. Black Form: ink/indigo/lilac with cream and pale highlights, smoke tendrils, dark flames and a black katana crescent (`black_form_slash_*`), poses from the painted black-katana concept reduced to pixel art (smoke stripped, 50-72 px tall) at x6, exits left.

## Lightning Form cut-in (v1, 2026-10-19, NOT wired into the game)

The full-screen "pop" for the right-click skill, built like the Black Form cut-in (`presentation/arena/black_form_cutin.gd`) so a sibling script can play it.
Generated by `python tools/art/export_lightning_cutin.py` (Pillow only, fixed seeds, re-running gives identical files) into `presentation/cutin/` next to the `black_form_*` files.
Everything is drawn at 1x from `concept_v1.png` in Totoy 36 with hard alpha and scaled up with integer nearest-neighbour. No gold or red.
Sizes are in the 800x450 cut-in canvas that `black_form_cutin.gd` draws at 0.4 on the 320x180 game canvas.

Suggested timing (seconds at 30 fps, as in the preview; about 1.05 s in total, faster than Black Form's 1.78 s):
- 0.00 `flash_1` (1 frame), 0.03 `flash_2` (2 frames), 0.10 `flash_3` (2 frames) while the banner slams in from the left (0.03-0.17, ease-back).
- 0.10-0.37 `crouch` with the aura (aura frame every 2 game frames, flipped every other), a `bolt_4` flicker every third frame from 0.20.
- 0.37 cut: `ready_sil` on a pale banner with radial lines (1 frame), then `ready` under a pale veil (1 frame); `ready` until 0.60.
- 0.60 cut: `burst_sil` + `bolt_1` + `flash_2`, then `bolt_2` over the whole screen for 2 frames with `flash_3`, `bolt_3` x2, `bolt_4` x2, then `bolt_3`/`bolt_4` flicker every 2 frames.
- 0.66 title slam "LIGHTNING FORM" (the title is font text drawn by the script, like Black Form; it is not in these files).
- 0.88-1.04 the banner dashes out to the right with speed-line smears (Black Form leaves to the left).
- Figure scale: the preview uses `FIG_SCALE` 0.63 like Black Form, times 1.0 / 0.92 / 0.8 for crouch / ready / burst so the tall burst pose stays on screen; the lit figure is drawn over the banner edge (breaks out at the top), the impact silhouette stays inside.

Known limits: the poses are the 64px concept frames at x12, so at game scale one art pixel is about 3 game pixels (chunky on purpose, unlike Black Form's painted poses). The preview's banner gradient, veils and title are drawn the way the runtime script would draw them, so they are not palette art.

189 sprites in total: 54 dagger hero (48 matching the hero2 frames + 6 for swing 4), 48 lightning hero, 85 effects, 2 icons.

How the hero frames are made: each `hero2_*` frame's katana is removed (water-ramp blade pixels, a body-colour fill where it crossed the body, the ink crumbs and a hilt crumb on `victory_1`), then two daggers are drawn at hand points measured by eye per frame (table `HANDS` in the script, with blade angle, layer and which hand leads). Far-hand dagger behind the body, near-hand dagger in front. Relaxed poses use a reverse grip with the blades down; parry is a crossed-dagger guard at the grip. The canvas only grows (in steps of 8) where a dagger reaches past it; then the anchor moves by the same amount (walk_2, walk_6, hurt_3, victory_1, attack2_4, attack3_2), so the feet still line up.

Since 2026-10-20 the bodies lean like the approved katana set: `import_dagger_sprites.py` builds each body with `tools/art/make_hero_lean_sheet.py` (`POSES`, plus `DAGGER_POSES` for the swing-4 bodies) from the upright frames in `art/reference/hero_lean/upright/`, and carries every `HANDS` point (measured on the upright frames) through the same warp; the blade angle turns with the torso there. `LEAN_HANDS` can override a frame (empty: every frame was checked on 9x grids and the daggers sit in the fists). Islands of at most 6 pixels left from the katana outline are dropped.
Attack swings: 1 front-hand thrust (speed streaks), 2 rising cut with the other hand (arc from below), 3 overhead-to-low cut (arc from above), 4 twin finisher: gather, dagger raised to the sky (cue for `fx_lightning_strike`), both daggers drive down with two arcs, settle. The striking dagger is one pixel longer in the active frames.
Lightning bodies use the approved concept look (`lightning_body` in the reference script: pale blue body, bright rim, inner bolt); daggers stay steel, speed arcs turn white.

Known limits: hand points are hand-measured on 1x frames (about +-1 px); swing 4 reuses bodies from other animations, so its active frames look like swing 3 with both daggers striking; the speed arcs are baked into the active frames (draw no extra sword trail over them, or remove them in the script). The sprite atlas grows to 256x6460 (under 8192). Motion, timing and whether it reads as fast were not judged in motion, only on still sheets.
