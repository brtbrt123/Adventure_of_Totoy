# Beacon epilogue: the arena restored, the beacon lit (reference art v1, for review)

Status: **v1 for the human's approval. Not imported into `art/src`, not in `art/build`, not wired into the game.** This is S6 of `docs/CHAPTER1_PLAN.md`: the human chose a cutscene on the restored arena ground, not a separate painting.

Generated by two scripts. Both use Pillow only and fixed seeds, so two runs give byte-identical files. Run the ground script first:

```powershell
python tools/art/make_arena_restored_ground.py   # presentation/arena/arena_ground_restored.png
python tools/art/make_beacon_epilogue_art.py     # this folder + docs/previews/arena_restored_v1.png, arena_before_after.png, beacon_ignite.gif
```

Everything uses Totoy 36 with hard alpha and never uses gold `x`,`y` or red `z`,`A`. The scripts check this: the sheet allows only opaque palette colours or full transparency, and the restored ground must be opaque and palette-only. Every sprite size is a multiple of 8. Translucency is faked with ordered (Bayer) dither.

## The restored ground (`presentation/arena/arena_ground_restored.png`, 800x480)

This is a palette-step edit of `arena_ground.png`. **No pixel is moved**, so every wall, pillar, rubble pile, the statue and the altar stay where the colliders in `core/arena_layout.gd` expect them. The script makes these edits:

- **Greens:** the forest ring goes up one ramp step and the clearing about 0.75 of a step. Which pixels step up is decided by their brightness against a 5x5 median of their neighbours, so the texture stays and flat areas get no checker. Ink inside the foliage becomes grass_1.
- **Light pool:** a soft pool of light around the platform (rx 330, ry 200). Stone and dirt take it in flat bands with a narrow dithered edge. Grass takes a little more, which gives warm yellow-green tops. On the platform, the brightest stone turns cream (`1`), with pale cyan (`p`) on its rim. The dark joints get less lift, so the contrast stays.
- **Rune ring:** the glyphs are one step brighter (teal goes to pale), and a teal glow bleeds onto each glyph's tile. A pale-teal halo sits on the grass just inside and outside the ring stones. The medallion's rim and its glyphs glow pale.
- **Moss and flowers:** moss creeps one pixel onto stone where 3 or more neighbours are already green. There are 16 small clusters of flowers and loose petals in the mossy spots on the stones, in cream, peach, lilac and pale cyan.

There are no pink petals in the palette, so the "pale pink-white" petal is cream `1` shaded with peach `s`.

Sorting: the beacon can go above everything in the cutscene. If characters walk around it, sort it by its anchor y (240). The pool and ripple belong under characters.

## Known limits

- The beam is pixel-straight and symmetric, and the shimmer is in its width, not its position. It reads as a calm pillar, not a flickering flame.
- The halo on the ring grass is 1-3 px of teal on each side of the ring stones. Up close it reads as a glowing rim. At 1x it is subtle.
- The flower clusters are tiny (1-3 px flowers). At 1x they read as specks of colour, not as individual flowers.
- On the restored ground the medallion's rim is a pale ring. The beacon base covers its centre once the beacon is lit.
- The north and south plaza stones outside the pool keep their tones. Only the centre and the greens are brighter, which leaves a slight vignette.
- No `.import` files are made here. Godot creates them on the next editor import.

## What I could not judge

Whether this looks beautiful and reads as "peaceful and restored" (the human approves the art). Whether the brighter forest still feels like deep forest. Whether the beam is too thin or too thick for the ending. How the ignite timing feels at the real window scale. Whether the fireflies and petals are visible enough at 1x. I checked only stills, crops at 2x-10x and the GIF frames. Nothing was seen in Godot.
