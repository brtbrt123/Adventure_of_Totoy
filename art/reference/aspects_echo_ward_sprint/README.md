# Echo Step + Stone Ward + Sprint (reference art v1, for review)

Status: **v1, approved by the human and imported into `art/src` (see "In-game import" at the end). Not wired into the game yet.** Durations, speeds, i-frames, stored-damage math, shockwave radius and cooldowns are owner/core decisions. The art follows the numbers in the request: the echo lasts about 2 s, the ward about 1 s with a shockwave ring of about 64 px, and Sprint lasts 3 s at double speed.

Generated by `python tools/art/make_aspect_echo_ward_sprint_art.py` (Pillow only, fixed seeds; two runs give byte-identical files). It reuses the helpers of `make_aspect_lantern_phantom_art.py` (trim, pack, checks, arena floor) and `make_dual_dagger_art.py` (grids, labelled sheet, icon ring). Everything uses Totoy 36 with hard alpha and no gold `x`,`y` or red `z`,`A`. Translucency is faked with checkerboard or ordered (Bayer) dither. Every sprite is checked: sizes are multiples of 8, rows are even, and only palette symbols appear. Every sheet PNG is checked: only opaque palette colours or full transparency.

## Naming rule for the per-frame overlays

The overlays are `fx_echo_shield_<F>_<p>` (p 1-4), `fx_sprint_aura_<F>_<p>` (p 1-6) and `fx_sprint_after_<F>_<k>` (k 1-3). `<F>` is the hero frame the game is drawing, for example `hero2_walk_right_3`, `totoy_dagger_dash_right_2` or `totoy_scythe_idle_right_1`, the same rule as `fx_phantom_glow_<F>_<p>`. Draw the overlay at the same position with the same mirroring, using its own `ax`/`ay` (the overlays are cropped, so the sizes differ, but the anchors line up). The phase is timed from the aspect's start and does not depend on the hero frame. **Covered:** `idle_right_1-4`, `walk_right_1-6`, `dash_right_1-4` of `hero2`, `totoy_dagger` and `totoy_scythe`. **Not covered:** attack, parry, hurt, victory, coil and lightning frames. If the name is missing, draw no overlay; for Echo Step, use the generic `fx_echo_bubble` instead.

## Suggested timelines

- **Echo Step:** on cast, play place 1-5 and dust over Totoy, then loop the anchor at that point. At about 2 s minus 320 ms, play countdown 1-4. On recall: vanish 1-5 at Totoy's position (hero hidden), trail plus head 1-4 from there to the echo, and arrive 1-5 at the echo. The anchor stops at arrive 1, and the hero is drawn at the echo from arrive 2. Bubble (or shield) 1-3 loops during the i-frames, then 4. Dust plays at arrive 2.
- **Stone Ward:** on cast, play ward_raise 1-4, with the barrier from raise 3, then the hold loop for about 1 s. For each absorbed hit: block 1-5 at the hit point, absorb 1-4 in place of the barrier, then store_<hits> over the barrier. On release: ward_drop 1-3, barrier and store off, then shockwave 1-8 and cracks 1-4 at the feet.
- **Sprint:** on cast, play start 1-5. For 3 s: aura on every frame, lines, dust and 3 afterimages while moving, and the wind loop. At the end: aura off, then end 1-4.

## Known limits

- The echo always shows the idle pose (`hero2_idle_right_1`), whatever Totoy was doing when he placed it, and with the katana. A per-pose or per-weapon echo would be a later step. Its lilac outline carries it on grass; the teal body checker is low-contrast on green.
- The ward hero frames reuse the idle bodies unchanged. The slab carries the read, not a new bracing pose. The slab hides the katana tip in the hold frames.
- The barrier is a side-view crescent in front of Totoy. For a hero facing up or down (a later 8-way view), there is no separate dome.
- The trail is drawn for 0, 45 and 90 degrees. Other angles need runtime rotation, which jags pixel lines.
- The shockwave canvas is 144x80. At 320x180 the full ring covers about 40% of the screen width, which is deliberate for a release, but its readability against the boss's own telegraphs is unchecked. It uses no gold or red, so it cannot be confused with a parry or warning cue.
- The afterimages overlap the hero when he moves slowly. They are meant for sprint speed only.
- The sprint start ring is drawn with dotted dashes and looks pointed at its ends in frames 3-4.
- The Godot `.import` files next to the other reference PNGs are not created here; Godot makes them on the next editor import.

## What I could not judge

Whether the looks are right (the human approves the art). Whether the echo reads clearly as "where I will return" during a fight. Whether the countdown flicker is noticeable enough at the real window scale. Whether the ward's crescent reads as a shield and not as a slash. Whether the 64 px shockwave feels powerful or just busy. Whether the sprint aura and afterimages read as speed and not noise over long play. I could not judge the timing and feel in motion either. Only the still sheets, crops at 1x-8x and GIF frames were checked; nothing was seen in Godot.
