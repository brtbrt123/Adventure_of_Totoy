# Intro cutscene (draft text, owner rewrites)

Played when the player clicks New Game, before the village. Edit `data/cutscenes/intro.json`; this page is a readable copy. Skippable; plays once per new game.

## 1. night  (picture `intro_01_night_of_lights`, effect fade_in, holds 5 s)

> Every year, on the Night of Returning Lights, six beacons answer one another across the valley.

## 2. dark  (picture `intro_02_dark_hills`, effect slow_zoom, holds 5 s)

> This year, only one answered.
> The rest stayed dark.

## 3. heartloom  (picture `intro_03_heartloom`, effect pan_down, holds 6 s)

> Warmth, rain and memory flowed through the valley by way of the Heartloom, an old living mechanism.
> Then the Heartloom failed.

## 4. weather  (picture `intro_04_strange_weather`, effect slow_zoom, holds 5 s)

> The seasons lost their order. Animals changed. Familiar paths began to lead somewhere else.

## 5. road  (picture `intro_05_totoy_on_the_road`, effect fade_in, holds 6 s)

> Totoy, a young Waykeeper, woke on a road he did not recognise, a broken beacon key in his hand.
> He could not remember the night it all went wrong.

## 6. lantern  (picture `intro_06_lanternfen_below`, effect pan_down, holds 6 s)

> Far below, one lantern still burned. Lanternfen. Home.

## 7. gate  (picture `intro_07_the_rune_gate`, effect fade_in, holds 5 s)

> The rune gate knew the key, even if he did not.
> It opened.

Limits: 110 characters per line, 3 lines per beat, 12 beats, 24 lines in all.
