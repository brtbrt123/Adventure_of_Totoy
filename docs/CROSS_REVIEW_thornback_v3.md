## What the core now does (`core/mob_field.gd`, `core/mob.gd`, numbers in `Tuning.MOBS`)

| Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|
| 1. Slam reach 125 | `stag_slam.reach` 50 -> 125; the cone telegraph is `reach + body radius` (145), the same object that applies damage, so warning and hit are identical. Half-angle, damage, windup, recovery unchanged. |
| 2. Charge 1.5x | `strike_speed` 4.4 -> 6.6, `strike_ticks` 36 -> 24 (158.4 px as before); windup 56 and width 30 unchanged. Movement is the swept `Collision.move_resolved` (3 px steps), so no tunnelling at the new speed. |
| 3. Eight roots | `stag_roots` has `sites: 8`. Site 1 is locked on the hero (as the single burst always was); the other seven are chosen deterministically from the mob id and the tick (an integer LCG, no random generator): free ground (17 px clear of props and the walkable edge), inside the world, 40 px away from exits, at least 40 px from each other, over the fight floor plus a 60 px margin. **Escape rule:** a candidate is rejected when it would leave the hero no free spot outside every burst (radius 34 + hero 6 + 2) within 70 px (walk 42 px in the 50-tick windup, or one dash). Eight `Telegraph`s with unique ids, all owned by the Stag, committed on the same ticks; `telegraph_snapshots()` returns all eight. One `attack_telegraphed` event carries `telegraph_ids` and `sites` (not eight events). A hero standing in ANY of the eight is hit once. Radius 34 and 50/5/40 timings unchanged; still phase two. |
| 4. Chant | New attack row `stag_summon` (phase 1, no damage, kind `none`): windup 90 ticks (horn glow), strike 10 (release), recover 50, cooldown 60. **No telegraph.** Events: `mob_summon_started` {mob_id, attack, cast_start_tick, cast_release_tick}, `mob_summoned` {mob_id, summoned_ids, positions}. Exactly five Glowmoths (`Tuning.MOBS[glowmoth]`: 20 HP, radius 5, red stationary dart line 110 long, 6 damage), placed on a ring 44-100 px around the Stag, on free ground, 28 px from the hero, away from exits, 20 px apart (the ring widens if needed, the count is always five). Policy: first cast possible 300 ticks after the Stag wakes, never while one of its moths lives, 900 ticks lockout after each cast. |
| 5. Regeneration | While the Stag is alive and at least one moth **it called** lives: +2 % of its maximum HP per second **in total** (5.2 HP/s at 260), accumulated as a fraction per tick, applied in whole HP, clamped at max. Not per moth (tested: one moth heals exactly like five). Unrelated Glowmoths (no `summoner_id`) do nothing. Stops the tick the last moth dies (regeneration runs after the hero's blows in the same tick, so the order is fixed), when the Stag dies (its moths are despawned), on encounter reset and on leaving the scene (the core is destroyed). One summon wave at a time, so the condition can never stack. Event `mob_healed` {mob_id, amount, hp, live_summons, source `summons`} batched once a second and when the regeneration stops. |

Summoned moths pay no EXP, drop or kill flag, do not count for the encounter's `alive`/`total` or for clearing it, and are removed when the Stag falls, the encounter clears or it resets.

## Snapshot fields (documented here because `docs/architecture.md` carries uncommitted edits of Codex)

Mob view dictionary, new keys: `summoner_id` (int, 0 for regular mobs; the Stag's mob id on its moths), `regen_active` (bool, on the Stag), `live_summons` (int, on the Stag), `cast_start_tick` and `cast_release_tick` (absolute ticks, -1 when not casting; kept after the release until the next attack starts), `guarded` (Warden). `attack_id` is `stag_summon` during the chant (state `windup`, then `strike` for the release, then `recover`). New events: `mob_summon_started`, `mob_summoned`, `mob_healed`; `attack_hit` carries `guarded` for a reduced hit on a guarded mob.

## Cross-review of the presentation (pass)

Checked against the core above: it reads one cached root telegraph per snapshot (works with eight unique ids and shared owner), draws the slam shock from the exact cone fields, reads `regen_active` and `summoner_id` as emitted, takes chant progress from `cast_start_tick`/`cast_release_tick`, spawns nothing, and never computes the heal. Comments:

1. **P3, chant release restarts.** In `mob_view.gd` the `summon` frames use `state_ticks / 6`, and `state_ticks` restarts when the state goes `strike` -> `recover`, so the first frames (1-2 during the 10 strike ticks) replay at the start of recover. Use `10 - remaining` or the tick since `cast_release_tick` instead of `state_ticks`.
2. **P3, dead branch.** `&"chant"` as a mob state never occurs; the core uses `windup` with `attack_id == stag_summon`. Harmless; remove it so nobody expects the state.
3. **P3, slam rubble placement.** Ground rubble is placed at most 36 px from the Stag while the cone now reaches 145 px. Cosmetic only; consider extra dust along the cone's far edge so the visible effect matches the warning.
4. **Note, Glowmoth attack.** The core Glowmoth uses a short red stationary line (6 damage) as its "shot"; there is no projectile simulation. The orb, trail and pop art is therefore unused until the owner wants a real projectile (then it is a core task with an agreed snapshot). `regen_active` stays true at full health while a moth lives; draw the links only if that is what you want to show.

Cannot judge: the look of any of it, whether eight roots at once and the heal feel fair, and the numbers (all first guesses). Open owner questions: the targeting policy (first burst on the hero, seven spread), the heal interpretation (2 % of max, whole HP, batched events), and whether the slam should now start from farther than 62 px (its cone reaches 145 but the Stag still begins it only within 62 px).
